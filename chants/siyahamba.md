<!-- titre: Siyahamba -->

Siya hamb' e kukhanyen' kwenkhos',
Siya hamb' e kukhanyen' kwenkhos'.
Siya hamb' e kukhanyen' kwe khanyen' kwenkhos'.
Siyahamba, hamba, siyahamba, hamba,
Siya hamb' e kukhanyen' kwenkhos'.

---

We are marching in the light of God,
We are marching in the light of God.
We are marching in the light of God, light of God.
Marching, marching, we are marching,
Marching, we are marching in the light of God.

---

Ons wandel in die lig van God,
Ons wandel in die lig van God.
Ons wandel in die lig van die, lig van God.
Wandel, wandel ons wandel,
Wandel, ons wandel in die lig van God.
